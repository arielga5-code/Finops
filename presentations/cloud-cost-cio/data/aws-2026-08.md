### The Marketplace line has changed character

The deck currently says of Marketplace: *"None of this is consumption. Security
vendors bought, replaced and retired inside the year."* That is no longer true.

| | July | August |
|---|---:|---:|
| One-off purchases | $142,960 (Cortex) | $0 |
| Recurring tooling | ~$13,700 | $14,738 |
| AI consumption | $9,188 | $44,739 |
| AI as a share of Marketplace | 5.5% | **75.2%** |

August contains **no one-off Marketplace purchases at all**. Every dollar of the
$59,477 is recurring, and three quarters of it is Claude consumption through Bedrock —
which is consumption in every sense except the line it bills on.

So "Marketplace" can no longer be treated as a single excluded bucket. It needs to
split three ways, because the three behave differently in a budget: one-off purchases
are lumpy and capital-like, tooling contracts are a flat monthly floor, and AI is the
fastest-growing consumption in the estate. Reporting them as one number both hides the
AI growth and overstates how much of the bill is "not consumption".

Note this also means AWS AI spend is understated wherever the deck sources it from
regular usage: $44,739 of it never appears there.
